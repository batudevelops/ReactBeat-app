Delete your ReactBeat account

ReactBeat, published by Batu Develops. This page explains how to request deletion of the account and game data we store for ReactBeat.

How to request deletion

  1. Email batu.develops@gmail.com with the subject “ReactBeat account deletion”.
  2. Send it from the Google or Apple email address you linked in ReactBeat, if you linked one.
  3. Include the display name shown on the ReactBeat home screen.
  4. Tell us you want the ReactBeat account and the data listed below deleted.

There is no delete button inside the app. Signing out does not delete the account. We reply to the email and delete the matched account within 30 days.

If you only played as a guest and never linked Google or Apple, include the display name. We can delete a guest profile only when that name identifies one account. If it matches more than one profile, we will ask for more detail before deleting anything.

Data we delete

  • The Firebase sign-in for that profile, whether it is a guest session or a linked Google or Apple sign-in.
  • The profile we store: display name, avatar choice, premium flag, dates, streak, games played, XP, and best scores.
  • Leaderboard rows for that profile, including the name and score shown on daily, weekly, and all-time boards.

Data we keep

  • Google Play and Apple keep the receipt for the one-time ReactBeat premium purchase. We do not refund that purchase from this request. The same store account can restore premium in the app later.
  • We keep the purchase record needed to honor that restore and to prevent a deleted profile from being used to fake a purchase.
  • We do not store your advertising ID in the account. Ads use the advertising ID on the device through Google AdMob. Reset that ID in your device settings. AdMob’s own copies are controlled by Google, not by this request.

ReactBeat does not request device location, and it does not collect health information, contacts, photos, or a password. Advertising partners may infer an approximate location from the IP address. The full account is described in the privacy policy.

Hesap silme (Türkçe)

ReactBeat hesabınızın ve oyun verinizin silinmesini istemek için batu.develops@gmail.com adresine “ReactBeat hesap silme” konulu bir e-posta gönderin. Google veya Apple ile bağladıysanız, bağlı hesabın e-posta adresinden yazın ve ana ekranda görünen görünen adı ekleyin.

Uygulamada hesap silme düğmesi yoktur. Çıkış yapmak hesabı silmez. Eşleşen hesabı 30 gün içinde sileriz: oturum, profil (görünen ad, avatar, premium işareti, seri, oyun sayısı, XP, en iyi skorlar) ve liderlik tablosundaki satırlar.

Google Play ve Apple, tek seferlik premium satın alma kaydını kendileri tutar. Bu talep o ücreti iade etmez. Aynı mağaza hesabı uygulamada premium’u yeniden yükleyebilir. Uygulama cihaz konumu istemez; sağlık, kişiler, fotoğraf veya şifre toplamayız. Reklam ortakları IP adresinden yaklaşık konum çıkarabilir. Ayrıntı gizlilik politikasındadır.
